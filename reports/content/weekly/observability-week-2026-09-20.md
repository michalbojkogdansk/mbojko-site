## Section 3 -- What's Moving in Observability - 2026-09-14 to 2026-09-20

_Week 13 of 13 in this collection cycle. This is the final data week before the Q3 report. Conference season peaked with Splunk .conf26 in Denver, while the rest of the market shipped quietly and consistently._

The defining move this week was Cisco using Splunk's annual conference to settle a question the market has been asking since the acquisition closed: what does Cisco actually build with Splunk? The answer is a data infrastructure play. Cisco Data Fabric converts machine data into AI-ready intelligence. The expanded NVIDIA partnership brings that to on-premises environments, where much enterprise observability data must stay for compliance or latency reasons. The multi-year AWS agreement co-developing security solutions adds a cloud counterpart.

### Cisco turns .conf26 into a product statement

[Cisco's September 15 announcements](https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m09/cisco-delivers-trusted-ai-at-scale-through-new-splunk-advancements.html) at .conf26 are the clearest articulation yet of the Cisco-Splunk combined platform. The Data Fabric positions Splunk as the intelligence layer between raw machine data and enterprise AI systems, not as a SIEM or a log aggregator. Federated Search for Snowflake means data does not have to move to be queried, which reduces the cost and latency penalty that has historically made observability data expensive to work with at scale. The NVIDIA partnership is the operationally significant piece: on-premises customers, which include most regulated industries, can now run Splunk AI models without sending data to a cloud provider. Jeetu Patel, Cisco's President and CPO, framed this as removing the "biggest roadblock to enterprise AI" -- deployment complexity.

### The quiet shift toward zero-toil observability

Two other launches this week point to the same operational pressure, from different angles. [Riverbed's NPM 360](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/17/riverbed-network-360-observability-solutions/) embeds Riverbed IQ's causal and generative AI directly inside AppResponse and NetProfiler, the products network operations teams already run. Current customers can activate it without additional cost. The value proposition is explicit: teams ask plain-language questions and receive AI-guided root-cause analysis without exporting data to a separate analytics layer.

[Elastic's cross-project search GA](https://ir.elastic.co/News--Events/news/news-details/2026/Elastic-Announces-General-Availability-of-Cross-Project-Search-for-Querying-Across-Serverless-Projects-Without-Moving-Data/default.aspx) on September 17 is a different kind of friction removal. Organizations running Elastic Cloud Serverless can now query across up to 100 linked projects in three clicks, with no certificates or remote cluster configuration required. Security analysts and SREs can now query across regions and teams without centralizing data first. The feature entered tech preview in April and reached GA on September 17.

[Dash0 shipped four updates on September 14](https://www.dash0.com/changelog/introducing-ai-sdlc-insights), continuing a pattern visible since July. AI SDLC Insights, the renamed and expanded version of AI Coding Insights, now maps the full AI-assisted development flow: agent costs, adoption by team, which tools and skills agents use, and how AI-assisted pull requests move from first prompt to merge. Agent0 model selection lets users choose the model per thread or automation. A GitHub Actions sync keeps dashboards and check rules in version control automatically. Dash0 has shipped more operational automation improvements than any other vendor this quarter.

The quarter's financials are complete: Datadog reported $1.12B in Q2 revenue (up 36%) in August, and Elastic reported $478M with a 23% stock jump on AI momentum. Dynatrace's ARR reached $2.14B with 17% growth in August. The Q3 results for calendar Q3 2026 will not arrive until October or November for most vendors, after this collection cycle closes.

Three threads have been visible for more than two weeks and are positioned to anchor the Q3 narrative. Cisco's use of .conf26 to define the Splunk data infrastructure story settles, at least for now, what the combined entity is selling. And the Dynatrace-Arize acquisition ($915M, closed in August), together with Elastic's Deductive AI acquisition, signals that AI model observability is now acquisition-stage valuable: vendors are buying the capability, not building it from scratch.
