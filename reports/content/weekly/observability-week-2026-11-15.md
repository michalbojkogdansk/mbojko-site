KubeCon North America ran November 9-12 in Salt Lake City. Two of this week's items trace directly to the event. Dash0 shipped continuous profiling to general availability on November 10. New Relic announced the public preview of its Autopilot Kubernetes Agent the same day, at Booth 159.

### Cribl adds Tero, its third acquisition in four months

Cribl announced the acquisition of Tero on November 10. The previous two were Orion (SIEM) and Bindplane (data pipeline management). Tero adds host monitoring, APM, federated observability, and AI-native observability. Tero co-founder Ben Johnson becomes VP of Observability at Cribl. Adam Cohen and Nihar Singhal also join to lead the observability roadmap. No purchase price was disclosed.

Cribl also appeared on the inaugural AI 100 list from Newcomer and Bessemer Venture Partners on November 12. The list covers 100 private AI companies, evaluated on market leadership, estimated valuation, operating metrics, and culture.

### Dash0 ships continuous profiling to GA

Dash0 acquired Polar Signals in August 2026. Twelve weeks later, it shipped continuous profiling to general availability at KubeCon. The profiler uses eBPF and runs on the Parca open-source project (originally developed by Polar Signals). It attaches to every process on a Kubernetes cluster without code changes. Polar Signals reported overhead below 1% in its own measurements. Pricing is $0.60 per 1 million samples.

### New Relic Autopilot Kubernetes Agent enters public preview

New Relic's Autopilot Kubernetes Agent entered public preview on November 10. The agent runs more than 90 diagnostic detectors across Kubernetes events, object descriptions, logs, and APM traces. It identifies root causes and generates remediation code for engineer review. The context engine underneath, New Relic Ground Truth, is built on OpenTelemetry-based data.

### Analyst recognitions

Coralogix announced on November 12 that Gartner named it a Market Shaper in the October 2026 Emerging Market Quadrant for AI FinOps Tools (Startup Vendors category, Gartner report dated October 28, 2026). The company cited a customer example involving approximately 19,500 coding-agent users tracked across Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Codex CLI, and Gemini CLI.

Riverbed announced the same day that GigaOm placed it in the Agile Optimizers quadrant in the 2026 GigaOm Radar Report for AIOps. The report highlighted Riverbed's governance of automated remediation, AI decision audit trails, and compliance alignment. Riverbed states its customers have run more than 500 million cumulative AI-driven automations (self-reported figure).

### Splunk advance notice: security advisories due November 18

Splunk published advance notice SVD-2026-1101 on November 11. It states that security advisories for seven Splunk products will be disclosed on November 18, 2026. The products listed for patching include Splunk Enterprise 10.6.1 (and backport versions), Splunk Enterprise Security 8.7.0, Splunk SOAR 8.8.0, Splunk Stream 8.1.7, and Splunk AI Assistant 2.0.0. No CVE IDs or CVSS scores are included in the advance notice. Full details arrive next week.
