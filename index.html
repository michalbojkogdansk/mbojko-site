04 / About

Background

15 years in software engineering leadership, 10 years in technical IT roles. I've built and scaled engineering organizations across Europe — working with teams in the USA, UK, France, Germany, Spain, Estonia and Poland.

I teach Data Engineering and Digital Transformation at university level, in both Engineering and MBA programmes. My courses combine theory with hands-on simulations, interactive tasks, and AI-assisted exercises.

I run a tech community focused on observability — bringing practitioners together around platform engineering, monitoring, and modern software reliability.

My focus is the intersection of technical excellence, organizational design, and learning at scale.
